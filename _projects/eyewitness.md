EyeWitness is a minimalist Progressive Web App that lets witnesses quickly record, save, and share video evidence from any device with a camera and web browser. Built with accessibility and reliability in mind, it works across a wide range of devices and network conditions.

**Stack:** JavaScript, HTML, CSS, Python, Docker
