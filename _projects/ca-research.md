A web tool for exploring all 256 elementary cellular automaton rules (Rule 30, Rule 110, and the rest). Highlight patterns in the evolution with regular expressions, customize colors, initial conditions, and grid size, and export high-quality PNGs. Works on phones and tablets.

**Stack:** TypeScript, CSS
