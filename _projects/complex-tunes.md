An interactive instrument for exploring the relationship between music and complex systems. Load a recording and see its frequency energy as a rotatable 3D delay-coordinate portrait, or go the other way and listen to the Lorenz system, with x mapped to pitch, y to stereo position, and z to brightness.

**Stack:** HTML, JavaScript, Web Audio
